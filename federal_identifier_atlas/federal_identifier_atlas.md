> A regex match is a candidate, not proof that an identifier was issued. The complete identity key is often `issuer namespace + normalized value + effective date/version + object type`.

## Scope and completeness

There is no closed, central list of every federal identifier. Agencies, courts, programs, portals, and individual offices create local identifiers continuously. This atlas therefore catalogs concrete high-value grammars plus explicit delegated/open classes where no government-wide regex exists.

This atlas deliberately includes both concrete grammars and **open/delegated classes** such as agency FOIA tracking numbers, FAINs, solicitation numbers, agency dockets, NARA local identifiers, and military service-court dockets. Those families are real identifiers but do not have a safe government-wide regex.

## Recommended processing contract

1. Preserve the exact `raw_value` and source span.
2. Generate a deterministic `normalized_value`; never overwrite the raw value.
3. Attach `family_id`, `issuer_namespace`, `object_type`, and any required context such as Congress, court, fiscal year, or publication edition.
4. Apply the candidate regex only as a lexical gate.
5. Run semantic checks: position rules, check digits, controlled-code lists, date ranges, and component consistency.
6. Resolve against the authoritative issuer/API when possible and record validator/source/version/timestamp.
7. Enforce privacy policy before indexing or displaying person/credential identifiers.
